# seed_words.txt - vocabulary for agent.py's session seed (make_session_seed).
# One word per line; lines starting with # and any word that is not pure
# lowercase ASCII letters are ignored by the loader, so comments are safe.
# Sources, 2026-09-04:
#   - Codenames core 400 (designer-curated concrete nouns with dense
#     associations), github.com/Gullesnuffs/Codenames wordlist-eng.txt
#   - wonderwords asset lists (the arxiv 2601.18053 source), used as a pool,
#     hand-filtered for concrete, imageable words
#   - readingvine.com sensory-word collection, hand-picked texture/sound words
#   - lexicraft.app rare-words collection (petrichor, apricity, ...), only
#     established or loan words kept, Dictionary of Obscure Sorrows coinages
#     (sonder, vellichor) left out
#   - the previous embedded _SEED_WORDS list from agent.py
#   - hand additions: weather, plants, animals, instruments, machines, craft,
#     anatomy, motion. Chosen for imageability and the feeling that two of
#     them together force a wrong-but-vivid connection.
